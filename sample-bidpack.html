Tender at a glance

Buyer
Nemocnice Pelhřimov, p.o. (Czechia); TED notice 783656-2026, ref. KYBER2026
Subject
Passive monitoring (CPS/OT/IoT) of at least 400 medical devices, hardware, licences, implementation, 60 months of support
Value
CZK 14,363,110 excl. VAT = 12,190,610 delivery + 2,000,000 support + 172,500 on-demand services (75 h, max CZK 2,300/h)
Procedure
Open, above threshold, EU-funded (IROP cybersecurity call). Offers in Czech via the E-ZAK portal with an e-signature
Deadline
21 December 2026, 09:00 CET. The original TED notice gave 11 December. The buyer extended the deadline in clarification no. 1. Check the buyer profile for further changes. Buyer profile and current deadline.
Award
Lowest total price excl. VAT. Every price cap above is an exclusion limit

The tender documents also specify basic eligibility and professional suitability. Check the criminal-record, tax and social security evidence, and the commercial register extract or equivalent under sections 8.2 and 8.3. Use the buyer’s qualification form where applicable.

What to check before pricing

The commercial terms and technical specification were revised on 19 November. Review the current versions of annexes 2 and 6 on the buyer profile. Buyer profile and tender documents.

  1. Licences must cover implementation and another 60 months from final acceptance. If rollout takes the full 12 months, allow for up to 72 months of coverage.
  2. The 400 devices are a minimum for medical equipment. In its clarification, the buyer says the monitored network segments contain more than 10,000 connected devices, and other devices cannot simply be excluded from licensing.
  3. The tender requires data collection from six sites. Verify the links and equipment needed at each site.
  4. Confirm integration with Tenable, Rapid7, Qualys and Check Point against the current technical specification and the buyer’s answers.
  5. Check the resource and retention limits and any conditions for handling metadata outside the hospital infrastructure.
  6. Review the delay, defect and incident penalties in the revised contract and reflect them in your pricing model.
  7. The buyer does not accept demo or refurbished medical equipment. Check the mandatory technical requirements against appendix 2.

Questions for the buyer

  • What device count and growth should the licence price assume above 10,000?
  • Which of the three scanner integrations must be demonstrated at acceptance?
  • How do delay penalties apply when the buyer provides network mirroring (SPAN) and access?

Section 13.3.3 says the buyer treats a request received by the eighth working day before the submission deadline as timely. Check the current deadline before sending a question through E-ZAK.

Related tender at a second hospital

Nové Město na Moravě, TED 783149-2026. The buyer profile lists 21 December 2026 at 10:00 CET. Section 8.4.1 uses “delivery or implementation”, while the Pelhřimov section uses “delivery and implementation”. Both reference templates and the Pelhřimov clarification use “or”, and both call for complete delivery and live operation or testing with positive results. Check the original documents for each tender and raise any ambiguity with the buyer.

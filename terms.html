Terms of Use (Pilot)

Version dated 23 November 2026

Team ByzForm (“we”) monitors IT procurement notices published on TED (Tenders Electronic Daily). These terms apply to the Weekly Digest, Scouting and Bid Packs. You can reach us at byzform@byzform.com.

1. Pilot phase

Features and formats may evolve during the pilot. The 3-month Scouting pilot period starts on the first day of the free 2-week trial. If you continue, the rate after the trial is €75 per month until the end of that period, after which the standard rate of €149 per month applies automatically. The Bid Pack rate is €245 for orders placed in the first 3 months as a pilot client and €490 per tender at the standard rate. We confirm which rate applies and the relevant dates by email.

2. Services

  • Weekly Digest is a free selection by country and field, sent by email. Reply “stop” to unsubscribe.
  • Scouting is a daily digest of notices matched to your company profile. We confirm the start of the free 2-week trial by email.
  • Bid Pack is a structured analysis of one tender’s documents, including a requirements matrix, checklist, clarification questions and a draft technical section. We agree the scope and delivery date by email before invoicing.

We confirm the language of your output by email. The Bid Pack’s draft technical section is prepared in the tender language.

3. Ordering and payment

Paid services are invoiced in EUR, excluding VAT, and paid in advance by bank transfer. We do not collect payment cards. To continue with Scouting after the free trial, confirm by email. Each month is then paid in advance. You can cancel Scouting by email at any time with effect from the following month. There is no minimum term. A Bid Pack is paid in advance once the order is agreed. We confirm the billing dates and amounts by email before paid service begins, including any billing period that spans the end of the pilot.

4. Nature of the information

Summaries, fit scores and bid/no-bid verdicts are our assessment based on public notices and the profile you give us. They are informational and do not replace your own review of the official tender documents. Deadlines, requirements and values must always be checked against the original notice on TED or the buyer’s platform. You remain solely responsible for the decision to bid, the content of your bid, pricing, legal declarations and submission.

5. Liability

We work with care but cannot guarantee that every relevant tender is found or that every summary is complete or error-free. To the extent permitted by law, our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us for the service in the 3 months preceding the claim, and we are not liable for lost profits or a lost or unsuccessful bid.

6. Confidentiality

We use information you provide about your company and bids to deliver the service to you and keep it confidential. Our email provider handles the correspondence used to deliver the service. We do not otherwise disclose your company or bid information to third parties or other clients. See our Privacy Notice for how we handle personal data.

7. Source data

Tender data comes from TED, © European Union, reused under the TED reuse policy. Links to original notices are provided in each digest.

8. Changes

We may update these terms during the pilot. Material changes will be announced by email before they take effect.

What helps you decide

Check the source

Notices link to TED so you can check the published deadline and documents. Scouting also tracks published changes and deadline extensions.

Check eligibility before you bid

Scouting compares what a notice asks for with your profile. It highlights relevant references, certifications or other conditions your team should verify.

Identify work that affects the price

A Bid Pack identifies requirements such as data migration, integration and ongoing support so your team can account for them in its bid.

Frequently asked questions

Which notices does ByzForm monitor?

We monitor public IT notices on TED with CPV codes beginning with 72 or 48. Check TED and the buyer’s procurement portal for the official notice and updates. If a particular country or secondary CPV code matters to you, contact us to check coverage before you start.

Is the Weekly Digest tailored to our company?

The free Digest is selected by country and field. Scouting adds an assessment against your company profile, with a recommendation and reasons.

Which languages are available?

English, Czech, Slovak, Polish and German. We confirm the language by email before the service starts. The Bid Pack’s draft technical section is written in the language of the tender.

When do I pay after the Scouting trial?

If you choose to continue after the 2-week trial, we confirm this by email and invoice you for the month ahead. You can cancel Scouting with effect from the following month. We confirm the billing dates and amounts by email before paid service begins, including any billing period that spans the end of the pilot.

How does the pilot pricing work?

We confirm whether the pilot applies, its start date and its scope by email. The 3-month Scouting pilot period starts on the first day of the free trial. If you continue, you pay €75 per month after the trial until the pilot period ends, after which the standard rate of €149 per month applies automatically. A Bid Pack ordered within the first 3 months of pilot participation costs €245. We invoice it in advance once you order.

Does a Bid Pack include bid submission?

A Bid Pack includes analysis and a draft technical section. Your team handles final pricing, declarations and submission.
